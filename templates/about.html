Importance of Time Management

Time is one of the most valuable resources. Proper time management helps individuals and organizations become more efficient and productive. A well-structured timetable allows for better planning, reducing stress and increasing focus.

How to Use a Timetable?

Creating a timetable involves identifying tasks, prioritizing them, and allocating time efficiently. Whether you're a student managing study sessions or a professional handling meetings, a proper schedule ensures optimal time utilization.
